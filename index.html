Supply checklist

Family continuity playbook

01
Water first

Store drinking/cooking water in clean, capped containers; rotate stocks.

02
Food without electricity

Stock ready-to-eat halal canned foods, crackers, oats and UHT milk.

03
Power & communications

Charge power banks, prepare LEDs and keep offline contact details.

04
Backup payments

Keep modest cash and more than one authorised payment method.

05
Check solar backup capability

Grid-tied solar usually shuts off in outages unless correctly equipped for backup/islanding.

LPG must be upright, secured and stored in a suitable ventilated location. Do not operate charcoal or camping burners indoors due to carbon monoxide risk.
